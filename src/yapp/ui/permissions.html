Yapp
Yapp needs three things from macOS
Two of them macOS won’t ask for. Yapp checks every 2 s; this window closes on its own when all three are on.
Microphone
to hear you · transcribed on this Mac, nothing leaves
Input Monitoring
to notice ⌥ Space while another app is in front
Accessibility
to open apps and type for you
In Settings: find Yapp in the list and switch it on. If it isn’t listed, press + and pick Yapp from Applications.
Press ⌥ Space. Yapp listens until you close it.
Words act as they lock in, so you can chain them: “open Notes and type shopping list”. Close with ⌥ Space again, Esc, or 4 s of quiet.
“open Notes”
any app by name. “switch to” works too.
“type …”
everything after “type” goes into the front app as you say it. “stop typing” ends it.
“open the budget file”
files by name from Desktop, Documents, Downloads and recents.
“run Morning”
any Shortcut by its name.
“undo”
reverses the last thing Yapp did.
Yapp never deletes, sends or pays. It will say “Won’t do that” instead.
